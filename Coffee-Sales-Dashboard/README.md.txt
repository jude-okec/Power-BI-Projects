Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Coffee Sales Analytics Dashboard built in Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and product preferences across multiple countries.

The report enables business users to:

Track sales, profit, and margin KPIs

Monitor Year-over-Year growth

Understand customer segmentation by coffee type

Evaluate the impact of loyalty programs

Identify geographic performance trends

Compare product profitability


Business Objectives

Improve visibility into overall sales performance

Identify high-profit coffee types

Analyze customer purchasing behavior

Measure loyalty program effectiveness

Track YoY profit growth

Key KPIs

Total Sales: $45K

Total Profit: $1,301

Profit Margin: 2.9%

YoY Profit Growth: 40.5%

Total Orders: 957

Active Customers: 913
