MexiToys Sales & Profit Performance Dashboard

Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides an end-to-end view of sales performance, profitability, and growth trends for MexiToys.
It enables business stakeholders to track KPIs, compare performance across locations and product categories, and monitor revenue vs. targets in a single interactive report.

The solution supports data-driven decision-making for commercial, finance, and executive teams.


Business Objectives

Monitor total orders, revenue, profit, and profit margin

Track month-over-month growth

Compare actual revenue vs. target

Identify top-performing product categories

Evaluate store location performance

Analyze revenue–profit trends over time
