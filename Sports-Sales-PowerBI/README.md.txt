Sports Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

Project Overview

This project delivers an interactive Sports Sales Performance Dashboard built in Power BI to monitor revenue, profitability, regional performance, product contribution, and sales channel effectiveness.

The report enables business users to:

Track KPIs in real time

Identify high-performing products and retailers

Compare operating margins across segments

Analyze sales trends over time

Make data-driven commercial decisions


Business Objectives

Provide a single source of truth for sales and profit metrics

Evaluate regional and product performance

Measure retailer contribution to total profit

Monitor operating margin efficiency

Compare sales channels (In-store, Outlet, Online)

Enable self-service analytics with dynamic filtering

Key Business Insights

West region is the highest revenue contributor.

Men’s Street Footwear leads product sales.

In-store sales dominate channel performance.

A small number of retailers generate a large share of operating profit.

High-revenue products do not always deliver the highest margins.
