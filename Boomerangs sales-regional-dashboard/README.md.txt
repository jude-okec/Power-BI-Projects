Sales & Regional Performance Dashboard – Power BI

Project Overview

This Power BI report delivers a comprehensive sales performance analysis across regions, products, and sales representatives.
It enables business users to track revenue trends, regional contribution, product performance, and transaction volume to support data-driven decision-making.

The solution is designed with a star-schema data model, time-intelligence measures, and drillable regional insights.

Business Objectives

Monitor overall sales performance

Compare regional revenue contribution

Evaluate sales rep effectiveness

Identify top-performing products by region

Track monthly sales trends

Analyze transaction volume growth
